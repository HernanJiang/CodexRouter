SQLite Public Domain Notice
===========================

The 2.0.0 Router stack stores its state in an embedded SQLite database
(router.db) through the rusqlite crate, which bundles the SQLite amalgamation.

SQLite is in the public domain and does not require a license:
https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html
